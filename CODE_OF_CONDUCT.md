# Code of Conduct

## Expected conduct

Participants must discuss mathematics and evidence with care and respect. Be precise
about errors without attacking the person who made them. Credit prior work, disclose
conflicts of interest, welcome good-faith correction, and make space for contributors
with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, sustained personal
attacks, plagiarism, fabricated evidence, and deliberate misrepresentation of a
claim's verification status are not acceptable.

## Mathematical disagreement

Strong criticism of an argument is appropriate when it is specific and supported.
State the exact inference, assumption, citation, or computation at issue. Maintainers
will preserve substantive objections in the public record and downgrade claims while
load-bearing disputes remain unresolved.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Maintainers
may edit or remove abusive material, lock a discussion, reject a contribution, or
temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions should be
proportionate, documented to the affected participant, and avoid disclosing a reporter's
identity without permission.

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and
project-linked community spaces.
